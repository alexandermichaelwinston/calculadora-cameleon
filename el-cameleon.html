01 · Oct 22 · Tariffs

Wind-turbine imports face Section 232 negotiations — no new tariff yet

What happened: An October 22 proclamation directed U.S. trade officials to negotiate over foreign wind turbines and components and report progress within 180 days; existing Section 232 duties remain unchanged.

Why it matters: Mexico and Canadian parts suppliers face policy uncertainty even before any new measure becomes effective.

Who may benefit / be hurt: Domestic component suppliers and customs specialists may gain; import-dependent developers may face risk.

Business move: Model existing HTS duties, supplier origin and potential scenarios separately; seek qualified North American substitutes.
Risks / verification: No new duty has been imposed by this proclamation. Verify later CBP instructions and any future tariff action.

02 · Oct 22 · Energy

Petroleum capacity receives potential Defense Production Act support

What happened: The October 22 White House determination makes production, refineries, pipelines, storage and terminals eligible for potential Section 303 actions.

Why it matters: U.S. Gulf infrastructure could see new financing or purchase support for strategically important projects.

Who may benefit / be hurt: Refinery contractors, tank builders and terminals could gain; projects that are not selected do not receive an advantage.

Business move: Prepare vendor qualifications for piping, tanks, automation, electrical and remediation; monitor actual procurement.
Risks / verification: This is no individual grant award, budget or construction authorization; verify future agency notices.

04 · Oct 22 · Peso

Banxico FIX reported at 18.7005 MXN/USD

What happened: A published FIX history shows 18.7005 pesos per dollar for October 22, compared with 18.6425 on October 21; the October 23 FIX was pending at this morning cutoff.

Why it matters: Currency swings change Mexican import costs, dollar debt burden, and dollar value of peso expenses.

Who may benefit / be hurt: Mexican dollar-paid exporters may gain; dollar borrowers and importers may lose.

Business move: Reprice cross-border quotes and model contracts around 18.3, 18.7 and 19.1 MXN per dollar.
Risks / verification: FIX is a reference rate, not an executable retail price; confirm Banco de México's official series before settlements.
