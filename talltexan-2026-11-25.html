NOV 24 · CALDWELL COUNTY

Daimler truck factory proposal targets at least 3,200 jobs

What happened: San Marcos is scheduled to consider a Chapter 380 incentive proposal December 1 and 15 for a potential two-million-square-foot truck plant. Other states are competing.

Why it matters: A plant of this scale could bring industrial construction, supplier and technician demand to the I-35 corridor.

Who may benefit or be hurt: Trades, equipment installers and parts makers may benefit; residents could face utility and traffic pressures.

Actionable business idea: Monitor council packets and vendor qualification; prepare civil, utility, electrical and fabrication credentials.
Risks / verification: No award or final site selection; verify annexation, wastewater capacity and performance-linked rebates.
San Antonio Express-News · November 24 ↗
NOV 24 · BRAZOS COUNTY

TSMC's proposed $22.6B fab remains incentive-dependent

What happened: Reuters reported that a TSMC subsidiary identified a property-tax limit as a key site-selection factor, with Arizona also under consideration.

Why it matters: Texas could gain advanced manufacturing capacity and high-spec industrial construction work.

Who may benefit or be hurt: Specialty contractors and technical workers could gain; taxpayers and utilities face potential costs.

Actionable business idea: Prepare cleanroom, process piping, water-treatment and electrical quality documentation for potential qualification.
Risks / verification: Proposal only. Check Comptroller JETI filings, final investment decision and contractor procurement; do not speculate on land based solely on an application.
Reuters · November 24 ↗
NOV 25 · TEXAS IMPORTERS

New Section 301 proposal puts material budgets under review

What happened: USTR proposed manufacturing-related tariffs against 14 economies today; a determination on Mexico remains pending.

Why it matters: Texas builders and manufacturers source equipment, fasteners, electronics and finishes globally and could see price changes.

Who may benefit or be hurt: Local component producers may gain; importers and fixed-price contractors could see margins compressed.

Actionable business idea: Update bill-of-materials origin records; seek alternative suppliers and review escalation clauses before signing long bids.
Risks / verification: Proposals are not current payable duties. Check HTS classification and existing tariffs with a licensed broker; January 5 comment deadline.
USTR · official proposal ↗
NOV 19 · SOUTHEAST TEXAS / LOUISIANA

Sabine Pass LNG expansion opens a regional vendor watchlist

What happened: FERC authorized three more liquefaction trains at the existing terminal in Cameron Parish, Louisiana, and related pipeline facilities.

Why it matters: Beaumont, Orange and Port Arthur are nearby industrial service markets; construction demand could cross the state line.

Who may benefit or be hurt: Fabricators, marine vendors and skilled trades could benefit; employers may face competition for qualified labor.

Actionable business idea: Track Cheniere and EPC procurement for pipe supports, insulation, electrical and industrial safety services.
Risks / verification: Louisiana project, not Texas construction expenditure. Authorization is not final investment approval or an awarded subcontract.
FERC · November 19 approvals ↗
NOV 21 REPORT · PECOS COUNTY

Data-center operator signs West Texas solar financial contract

What happened: The Midland Reporter-Telegram reported Sabey's ten-year virtual power purchase agreement for 43.5 MW of capacity at the operating Greasewood project.

Why it matters: Long-term energy contracting may help large commercial users manage financial exposure and sustainability goals.

Who may benefit or be hurt: Energy developers and advisers may benefit; buyers face basis and price risk.

Actionable business idea: Offer energy metering, maintenance and procurement advisory services to industrial users.
Risks / verification: A VPPA is a financial arrangement, not dedicated physical delivery or new solar construction; verify pricing, generation and settlement terms.
Midland Reporter-Telegram · agreement ↗
OCTOBER DATA · TEXAS JOBS

Texas employment reaches 14.50 million

What happened: TWC reports 14,500,500 seasonally adjusted nonfarm jobs in October, 203,300 above October 2025, a 1.4% annual gain.

Why it matters: A growing workforce supports housing and business services but may also compete for specialized trades.

Who may benefit or be hurt: Recruiters and training providers may benefit; contractors with scarce skills could face wage pressure.

Actionable business idea: Use regional wage data to price bids and partner with community colleges for apprenticeship pipelines.
Risks / verification: Statewide net employment is not a count of open vacancies. Check occupation and metro data; figures can be revised.
Texas Workforce Commission · October labor data ↗
NOV 13 DATA · BORDER LOGISTICS

Laredo and Ysleta lead the reported port-value table

What happened: BTS reported September freight values of $38.09 billion at Laredo and $15.77 billion at Ysleta across modes.

Why it matters: High-value corridors support customs, secure storage and time-sensitive shipments.

Who may benefit or be hurt: Warehouse operators and carriers may benefit; smaller shippers may struggle with delays or insurance costs.

Actionable business idea: Pitch bonded or secure storage, appointment scheduling and bilingual dispatch near existing distribution nodes.
Risks / verification: Port values are not local revenues or vehicle counts. Verify traffic, zoning and signed tenant demand before leasing.
BTS · September release ↗
NOV 25 CHECK · FOREIGN-TRADE ZONES

Garwood, Wilmer and Harlingen cases remain on Commerce list

What happened: Garwood production comments close December 7; Samsung Electro-Mechanics' Wilmer subzone comments close December 14; Penn Aluminum Harlingen production comments close December 23.

Why it matters: These cases show potential customs planning for industrial facilities in three Texas markets.

Who may benefit or be hurt: Brokers and plant compliance specialists may benefit; operators without adequate controls face costs.

Actionable business idea: Contact the relevant grantee and develop a landed-cost and inventory-control analysis for each operation.
Risks / verification: A public notice is not approval or a construction tender; check the controlling docket and specific production scope.
U.S. Commerce · open FTZ cases ↗
DEC 1 DEADLINE · TEXAS INCENTIVES

Enterprise Zone nominations and new Opportunity Zones require distinct planning

What happened: Texas lists December 1 for the next Enterprise Zone nomination round; Treasury designated 608 Texas census tracts for Opportunity Zone 2.0 effective January 1, 2027.

Why it matters: The first program involves local nomination for qualifying projects; the second is a federal investment tax framework.

Who may benefit or be hurt: Qualifying employers and eligible investors may benefit; projects that miss requirements or deadlines may lose expected savings.

Actionable business idea: Contact local economic development officials immediately; separately screen parcels against the new census-tract map with a tax adviser.
Risks / verification: No automatic rebates or capital-gains relief from location alone. Verify eligibility, investment structure, job obligations and deadlines.
Texas Enterprise Zone portal ↗Governor · Opportunity Zones ↗
GRANT WATCH · PORTS AND RAIL

Texas cargo-equipment grants accept applications while funds remain

What happened: TCEQ lists March 12, 2027 at 5 p.m. Central as the closing time for SPRY grants to upgrade or replace eligible older equipment.

Why it matters: Modernization could cut emissions and improve reliability at qualifying ports and rail yards.

Who may benefit or be hurt: Eligible owners and equipment suppliers may gain; operators outside program criteria may not qualify.

Actionable business idea: Inventory older drayage trucks and cargo equipment, assemble usage records and request quotes before applying.
Risks / verification: First-come, first-served. Confirm eligible counties, ownership and operating history, available funding and cost-share rules before purchasing.
TCEQ · SPRY grant page ↗
