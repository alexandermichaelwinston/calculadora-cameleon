1 · Midland · Aerospace

$5M grant puts a vertical launch range on the Permian Basin map

What: Midland approved a $5 million Texas Space Commission SEARF grant supporting site evaluation, design, licensing and foundational infrastructure for a vertical launch and reentry range from FY2027–FY2030.

Texas impact: Aerospace infrastructure can diversify West Texas beyond oil and gas and create demand for civil construction, electrical systems, telemetry, safety systems, specialized logistics and defense-adjacent services.

Operator move: build a vendor-readiness list around site civil, power, communications, security, environmental work and specialized trucking.
The grant funds development work, not a guaranteed operating launch complex; licensing and private capital remain critical.
Midland Reporter-Telegram · Sep. 2
2 · Forney · FTZ + Battery Supply Chain

Hithium’s proposed FTZ subzone reaches a Sep. 8 comment deadline

What: Hithium Tech USA has applied for subzone status under FTZ 168 for a 44.58-acre Forney facility; the current public-comment period closes Sep. 8.

Texas impact: A battery-related FTZ node can deepen Dallas-area logistics, component sourcing and energy-storage manufacturing while creating customs and warehousing demand.

Operator move: pitch bonded logistics, inventory systems, packaging, maintenance and local component capacity; also model FTZ treatment for adjacent import-heavy operations.
Subzone approval is pending and actual savings depend on imported inputs, tariff status and customs controls.
Federal Register · Hithium FTZ application
3 · New Braunfels · Automotive

CGT seeks FTZ production authority for automotive upholstery

What: CGT US Ltd. has a pending FTZ 80 production request in New Braunfels for PVC-coated supported automotive upholstery; comments close Sep. 14.

Texas impact: The filing shows tariff engineering is becoming part of automotive manufacturing strategy as firms manage Section 301 exposure and imported chemicals/materials.

Operator move: if you supply auto interiors, plastics, coatings or chemicals, map your own duty exposure and look for U.S./Mexico substitutes with cleaner tariff treatment.
FTZ status does not erase all tariffs; certain Section 301 goods must enter in privileged foreign status.
Federal Register · CGT FTZ notice
4 · Irving · Medical Devices

Abbott’s Irving FTZ filing opens a medtech supplier opportunity

What: Abbott Laboratories has proposed FTZ production activity in Irving for diagnostic medical devices using a broad mix of imported plastics, electronics, sensors, motors, pumps and metal components; comments are due Sep. 15.

Texas impact: Medtech production can create local demand for precision machining, plastics, cable harnesses, electromechanical assemblies, QA and regulated logistics.

Operator move: target components that can be localized without triggering costly revalidation, and build ISO/quality credentials before outreach.
Medical-device supplier qualification is rigorous; FTZ treatment and Section 232/301 rules are component-specific.
Federal Register · Abbott FTZ notice
5 · San Antonio · Power Equipment

IEM’s $200M expansion still points to a 3,000-job supplier hunt

What: Industrial Electric Mfg. is establishing an advanced-manufacturing facility at Brooks with $200 million of investment and up to 3,000 jobs; Texas extended a $3.36 million TEF grant tied to 448 jobs.

Texas impact: Switchgear and power-distribution demand is tied directly to factories, data centers and grid expansion.

Operator move: pursue enclosures, copper, busbar, wire harnesses, testing, controls, field service, trucking and technical staffing.
“Up to” jobs may phase over years; focus on awarded production scope and supplier qualification.
Texas Governor · Aug. 25
6 · Grimes County · Semiconductors

Terafab remains Texas’ biggest tier-2 / tier-3 supplier map

What: SpaceX plans a vertically integrated semiconductor fab in Grimes County; first-phase investment exceeds $16.8 billion with 3,000 jobs, a $30 million TEF grant and JETI qualification.

Texas impact: Fabs create demand far beyond chips: cleanrooms, substations, water treatment, specialty gases, automation, packaging, housing and industrial real estate.

Operator move: identify suppliers that need satellite operations within a few hours of Grimes County and pre-position industrial sites with real power and water.
Later phases are not guaranteed; utility interconnection and construction sequencing can change the supplier calendar.
Texas Governor · Aug. 6
7 · San Antonio · Automotive

Toyota’s $3.6B second line raises the value of Texas/Mexico dual sourcing

What: Toyota is expanding its San Antonio assembly footprint with a second vehicle line, $3.6 billion of capital investment and 2,000 jobs; the project carries TEF support and JETI qualification.

Texas impact: With North American auto tariffs under debate, a Texas assembly anchor creates opportunities for suppliers that can serve both U.S. and Mexican production with compliant regional content.

Operator move: review Toyota-adjacent parts you can manufacture or warehouse within a one-day truck radius, including Monterrey and Texas suppliers.
OEM sourcing cycles are long and qualification-heavy; tariff proposals can change before production ramps.
Texas Governor · Jul. 6
8 · Gulf Coast · Shipbuilding

Davie Defense gives Port Arthur and Galveston a 2,400-job industrial anchor

What: Davie Defense is expanding shipbuilding capacity in Galveston and Port Arthur, with more than $730 million of capital investment, more than 2,400 jobs and a $21.771 million TEF grant.

Texas impact: Specialized shipbuilding supports steel, welding, coatings, marine electrical, cranes, machining, logistics and skilled-trades training across the Gulf Coast.

Operator move: build maritime qualifications, bonding capacity, QA/QC and a list of shipyard-ready labor before procurement peaks.
Defense and commercial ship schedules depend on contract awards, federal procurement and yard milestones.
Texas Governor · Feb. 11
9 · Statewide · JETI + OZ 2.0

Industrial-site math is increasingly about incentive stacking

What: JETI provides qualifying projects a 10-year school-district M&O appraised-value limitation, with job and investment thresholds tied to county population. Texas is also preparing for the next Opportunity Zone cycle beginning in 2027.

Texas impact: A strong site can combine JETI, Opportunity Zone treatment, local abatements, workforce grants and potentially FTZ economics — but only if the project and location qualify.

Operator move: build a site matrix that includes power, water, labor and logistics first, then layers verified incentive value on top.
Never underwrite an incentive before formal designation/approval; OZ and JETI rules are separate and documentation-heavy.
Texas Comptroller · JETITexas OZ 2.0
10 · Texas Operators · Labor + Cost Pressure

Manufacturing jobs fell nationally while oil and yields climbed

What: ADP reported U.S. manufacturing employment down 17,000 in August, while construction added 12,000. On Sep. 2, higher oil prices and bond yields also lifted borrowing and input-cost pressure.

Texas impact: Texas manufacturers may still have project demand, but margin management matters more when labor productivity, financing, freight and materials move in different directions.

Operator move: tighten bid-validity periods, fuel/escalation clauses, overtime controls and machine-utilization reporting before Q4 work is priced.
ADP data can be revised and oil/yield moves may reverse; protect contract economics instead of betting on a macro forecast.
Reuters · Sep. 2 payrollsReuters · Sep. 2 markets
