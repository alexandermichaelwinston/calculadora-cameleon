Important: This MVP records electronic consent, signer identity fields, timestamp, request metadata and a SHA-256 hash when the PDF can be fetched. A production e-signature product should also include authenticated accounts, tamper-evident completed PDFs, retention controls, consent/consumer-disclosure flows, and jurisdiction-specific legal review before marketing signatures as universally “legally binding.”
