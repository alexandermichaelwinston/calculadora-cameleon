# Business YouTube 100™ — Week ending November 20, 2026

El Camaleón × The Tall Texan · Nine curated selections from seven publishers · Release window November 16–20, 2026.

**Method:** Selected for operator usefulness, not quantity. Not a claim that 100 channels were individually audited; video/podcast formats and provenance are labeled. Publisher reports, interviews, and predictions are not investment guarantees.

## 1. Lead to Profit: Use AI and JobTread to Turn Marketing Dollars Into Booked Jobs
**Publisher/date:** JobTread · Scott Schaper (RSM Marketing) · Nov 18, 2026
**Category:** AI · Recorded contractor webinar
**Main idea:** Missed calls, delayed follow-up, scattered CRM records and weak attribution waste contractor ad budgets. The webinar connects marketing, sales and job-cost data to booked work.
**Why it matters:** For Alex Pros and Rocket-Launcher.io, recovering existing inquiries can be more profitable than increasing ad spend.
**Practical takeaway:** Audit the last 50 inquiries: response time, estimate issued, booked job and gross profit. Pilot supervised missed-call follow-up and an estimate-to-job dashboard.
**Watch/source:** https://www.jobtread.com/webinars/partner-spotlight-lead-to-profit-use-ai-and-jobtread-to-turn-marketing-dollars-into-booked-jobs
**Additional context:** https://www.jobtread.com/webinars

## 2. Home Prices Just Ended a 13-Year Streak | November Housing Update
**Publisher/date:** BiggerPockets · Dave Meyer · Nov 20, 2026
**Category:** Real estate · Real-estate podcast / video
**Main idea:** Dave Meyer argues that a slight national year-over-year price decline, slow transactions and higher borrowing costs are improving buyer leverage without necessarily producing a 2008-style forced-sale crash.
**Why it matters:** Texas markets differ sharply by ZIP code and builder competition; falling ask prices do not rescue bad holding costs.
**Practical takeaway:** For each Real Deals buy box, record closed-sale discounts, active inventory, concessions, insurance, taxes and 12-month carry. Run a second case with 5% lower resale value.
**Watch/source:** https://www.biggerpockets.com/blog/real-estate-1359
**Additional context:** https://podsdaily.com/podcast/biggerpockets

## 3. (Please) Don’t Bet on a Refinance
**Publisher/date:** BiggerPockets · Michael Zuber, Dave Meyer, Henry Washington · Nov 18, 2026
**Category:** Real estate · Publisher transcript / podcast
**Main idea:** The guests argue that a rental should work at today's financing terms instead of relying on later refinancing or appreciation; property type changes vacancy and maintenance assumptions.
**Why it matters:** Owner finance and conventional rentals alike can become cash traps when optimistic rates or generic expense estimates hide negative cash flow.
**Practical takeaway:** Approve offers on present-day debt service and reserves. Treat future refinancing as upside only; maintain different expense models for single-family, 2–4 unit and apartment assets.
**Watch/source:** https://www.biggerpockets.com/blog/real-estate-1358

## 4. The AI Bubble Already Popped
**Publisher/date:** The Compound & Friends · Jenny Harrington, Bryn Talkington · Nov 20, 2026
**Category:** Investing · Publisher podcast
**Main idea:** The panel debates an AI-stock selloff, changing leadership among technology and dividend stocks, and how to position amid a shifting AI investment cycle; provocative headlines are discussion, not established forecasts.
**Why it matters:** Portfolio exposure to AI, construction demand, technology customers and credit conditions can overlap more than it appears.
**Practical takeaway:** Draw an exposure map across public stocks, private apps, real estate and AI-dependent contractors; cap any single economic dependency and test a slower-capex case.
**Watch/source:** https://podcasts.thecompoundnews.com/show/TCAF/the-ai-bubble-already-popped-with-jenny-harrington-and-bryn-talkington/

## 5. $1.5B+ Investor: “This Is Why Elon Wins”
**Publisher/date:** My First Million · James Currier / Shaan Puri · Nov 16, 2026
**Category:** Entrepreneurship · Podcast interview
**Main idea:** Investor James Currier argues that faster product cycles, tighter deadlines and quicker customer validation help founders discover viable opportunities sooner than careful but slow planning.
**Why it matters:** Multiple software and commerce brands can consume energy without generating evidence of product-market fit.
**Practical takeaway:** Choose one offer per brand; set a seven-day test with an actual buyer, conversion target and stop/iterate decision. Speed means faster validated learning, not bypassing safety.
**Watch/source:** https://podfollow.com/my-first-million/episode/7403baa611b074fb5441fbdf724d700afb1be3e9/view
**Additional context:** https://podcastrex.com/shows/my-first-million/15b-investor-this-is-why-elon-wins

## 6. She’s Making $40M Selling a Game to Rich Moms
**Publisher/date:** My First Million · Sam Parr / Shaan Puri · Nov 18, 2026
**Category:** Entrepreneurship · Verified YouTube video
**Main idea:** The hosts use luxury mahjong products and high-priced eyewear to explore community, distinctiveness and premium pricing; the $40M figure is a creator-reported claim, not audited revenue.
**Why it matters:** Texas Merch and Mikey Shoes need distinctive full-set presentation, community and repeat purchasing—not just more SKU counts.
**Practical takeaway:** Test two premium family-set landing pages against ordinary single-item listings, comparing add-to-cart rate, average order value, gross margin and purchase conversion.

## 7. GM to Increase Factory Robot Use by 20%
**Publisher/date:** Manufacturing.net / Industrial Equipment News · Nov 18, 2026
**Category:** Manufacturing · Verified YouTube video
**Main idea:** A report says GM is targeting more factory robots by 2028, particularly autonomous material-moving equipment, although the specific target was not independently confirmed by GM.
**Why it matters:** Factory automation creates opportunities in integration, controls and maintenance while introducing labor, safety and reliability tradeoffs.
**Practical takeaway:** Evaluate automated material movement by cost per moved unit, downtime, uptime, training and worker safety. Build a shortlist of Texas suppliers only after confirming procurement demand.
**Watch/source:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gGVLcmvViGs
**Additional context:** https://www.manufacturing.net/automotive/video/22978632/gm-to-increase-factory-robot-use-by-20

## 8. Field of Dreams: Siding, Airtightness and Builder–Manufacturer Coordination
**Publisher/date:** The Build Show Network · Nov 17, 2026
**Category:** Construction · Build Show video collection
**Main idea:** A South Dakota high-performance build documents exterior cladding, window interfaces, ventilation and blower-door testing with builder and manufacturer representatives.
**Why it matters:** The transferable lesson for Gulf Coast contractors is field documentation and coordinated water/air-control details, not copying a cold-climate assembly unchanged.
**Practical takeaway:** Require manufacturer-approved flashing/WRB details and pre-cover photos at windows, cladding penetrations and roof transitions. Verify climate-specific assemblies with the project designer.
**Watch/source:** https://buildshownetwork.com/library/field-of-dreams-project-in-south-dakota-with-brandon-from-diamondkote

## 9. El Peso Se Depreció 2.26% y Sheinbaum Dialoga con Trump en APEC
**Publisher/date:** El Economista · La Primera · Nov 19, 2026
**Category:** Mexico trade · Publisher video · Español
**Main idea:** The Spanish-language news video covers a sharp peso decline and Mexican–U.S. diplomatic discussions, demonstrating how currency and trade expectations change quickly.
**Why it matters:** A changing USD/MXN rate can move the true landed cost of Mexican goods even before customs, freight or USMCA treatment are considered.
**Practical takeaway:** Use 18.0, 19.0 and 20.0 MXN/USD sensitivities in every supplier quote; separately verify HS classification, origin and which party holds FX risk.
**Watch/source:** https://www.eleconomista.com.mx/videos/peso-deprecio-2-26-mayor-caida-dia-abril-2025-sheinbaum-sostiene-dialogo-trump-marco-apec-20261119-844759.html
**Additional context:** https://www.eleconomista.com.mx/estados/motores-manufactureros-mexico-siguen-apagados-20261120-844947.html
