## 7. How Do I Flash a Window Properly?
**Publisher/date:** The Build Show Network · Nov 12, 2026
**Category:** Construction
**Format:** Publisher construction lesson

## El Camaleón — Americas lens
The most actionable cross-border signal is the combination of Mexico's auto-parts supply base, electrical-equipment investment and renewed tariff uncertainty. The WEG announcement is **not** a purchase order. A tariff threat is **not** a new customs rate. Price only after HS-code and USMCA-origin verification and a stress-tested USD/MXN landed-cost quote.

## The Tall Texan — Texas lens
Demand-side headlines are not enough. For real-estate acquisitions, underwrite builder incentives, property taxes, insurance, leasing demand, and carry. For contracting, make drainage details and field-office handoffs documented standards. For supplier work, track approved vendor opportunities rather than announced projects.
