## 1. When AI Agents Use Computers Faster Than You

- Date: Oct 26, 2026; Category: AI; Publisher: a16z Show · Ari Weinstein & James Sun; Format: Podcast episode
- Main idea: Computer-using agents are getting faster and more capable at working inside existing business software, including systems without dedicated APIs.
- Why it matters: AI could automate legacy finance, CRM and procurement workflows, but unreviewed screen actions can still create financial or security errors.
- Practical takeaway: Pilot one read-only CRM reconciliation; record correct completions, exceptions, and human-review time before permitting writes.

## 2. The Bulls Are Trapped: Positioning, AI Debt and Market Risk

- Date: Oct 30, 2026; Category: Investing; Publisher: The Compound & Friends · Alex Altmann; Format: Publisher audio/video episode
- Main idea: Tactical positioning, household equity ownership and rising AI infrastructure financing costs are changing market risk.
- Why it matters: Operators financing construction, data centers or acquisitions cannot treat a rising equity market as evidence of affordable project debt.
- Practical takeaway: Stress-test financing costs and refinancing dates; require one liquidity reserve and a downside demand scenario for major investments.
- Source: https://podcasts.thecompoundnews.com/show/TCAF/the-bulls-are-trapped-with-alex-altmann/

## 3. The New FIRE Movement That Includes Rentals

- Date: Oct 28, 2026; Category: Real estate; Publisher: BiggerPockets · Dave Meyer & Paula Pant; Format: Video/podcast with transcript
- Main idea: Paula Pant argues that a modest portfolio of paid-off rentals can support optionality without endlessly accumulating properties.
- Why it matters: Rental ownership must be judged by resilient cash flow and the owner's whole financial picture, not door count.
- Practical takeaway: For each rental, compare net cash flow under current debt, accelerated payoff and all-cash scenarios, including taxes and repairs.
- Source: https://www.biggerpockets.com/blog/real-estate-1349

## 6. Mexico Exports and Imports Surge in September

- Date: Oct 27, 2026; Category: Mexico trade; Publisher: El Economista · Mexico trade desk; Format: Publisher news video
- Main idea: The publication reports September merchandise exports up 37.9% year on year and imports up 33.1%, with nonautomotive manufactured goods a major driver.
