## 1. 4 business ideas you could build with ChatGPT

## 2. Why I’m Moving (More) Money Into Passive Real Estate in 2027
BiggerPockets · Paul Shannon | Nov 25, 2026 | Real estate | Publisher podcast / transcript

**Main idea:** An experienced sponsor and passive investor examines syndication structures, distressed commercial property and sponsor selection; a rebound is his thesis, not a certainty.

**Why it matters:** Passive ownership reduces management work but adds illiquidity and sponsor risk.

**Practical takeaway:** Build a diligence sheet for debt maturities, sponsor realized returns, fees, waterfall, reserves and downside scenarios.

[Publisher source](https://www.biggerpockets.com/blog/real-estate-1361)

## 5. EU propone nuevos aranceles a 14 socios comerciales por exceso de capacidad
El Economista | Nov 25, 2026 | Mexico trade | Publisher video · Español

**Main idea:** A Spanish-language report covers proposed U.S. duties aimed at excess industrial capacity among trading partners.

**Why it matters:** New trade barriers could change relative sourcing economics; proposals are not effective tariff schedules and Mexico-specific exposure needs separate verification.

**Practical takeaway:** Ask a customs broker to verify HS codes, origin, effective dates and alternative supply routes before approving a purchase.

[Publisher source](https://www.eleconomista.com.mx/videos/eu-propone-nuevos-aranceles-14-socios-comerciales-exceso-capacidad-20261125-845776.html) · [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BLmp-tVmHJA)

## 9. Hay apertura al diálogo para un incremento al salario mínimo de al menos 10%: Conasami

**Main idea:** Mexico’s minimum-wage commission president discusses a possible general increase of at least 10% for 2027, with a potentially smaller northern-border adjustment; negotiations are not final.
