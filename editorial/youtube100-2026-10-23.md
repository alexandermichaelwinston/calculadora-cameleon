### 1. a16z Show · Adit Abraham — The Missing Infrastructure for AI Agents: Documents
**Published:** Oct 21, 2026 | **Category:** AI | **Format:** Publisher episode

**Main idea:** AI agents can reason well but still miss critical data in scanned documents, messy PDFs, tables and spreadsheets.

**Why it matters:** A missed line in a contract, insurance file or construction estimate can turn an impressive demo into a costly production error.

**Practical takeaway:** Benchmark document extraction on 25 real bid sheets, contracts and invoices. Record omitted fields and require review before approvals or payments.

### 2. The Compound & Friends · Jill Schlesinger — An Economy So Good No One Can Live In It
**Published:** Oct 23, 2026 | **Category:** Investing | **Format:** Publisher episode

**Main idea:** Housing affordability and strained household sentiment can coexist with strong aggregate economic indicators and enthusiasm for AI investment.

### 3. Builder Stories · JobTread · Stephan Sardone — The Seminary Student Who Built a Luxury Remodeling Firm
**Published:** Oct 21, 2026 | **Category:** Construction | **Format:** Video + transcript

**Main idea:** A Texas remodeler details growing too fast, owner burnout, change orders absorbed without documentation and eventually splitting sales/production leadership.

**Why it matters:** Profitable growth requires reliable scopes, change-order controls and production capacity—not just signed contracts.

**Practical takeaway:** Require signed change orders before extra work; split estimating and production accountability; audit three recent bids for omitted scope.

**Watch/source:** https://www.jobtread.com/builder-stories-podcast/episodes/the-seminary-student-who-built-a-luxury-remodeling-firm

### 4. BiggerPockets Real Estate · Henry Washington — Was Dave Ramsey Right About Rentals? (Don’t Use Debt)
**Published:** Oct 21, 2026 | **Category:** Real estate | **Format:** Publisher episode

**Main idea:** A practical debate about buying rentals with cash versus financing, including shorter loans, tenant selection and operational headaches.

**Practical takeaway:** Compare all-cash, 15-year and 30-year financing cases with PITI, vacancy, maintenance, debt coverage and minimum cash reserves.
