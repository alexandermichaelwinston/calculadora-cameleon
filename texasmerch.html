STORAGE + FULFILLMENT

Build the warehouse system before volume becomes a problem.

The model needs organized storage because inventory is sourced in batches, held for subscriptions and bulk orders, then picked and packed into standardized cartons. Start lean, but design the workflow so a small warehouse can scale.

1 · Source in batches

Buy proven SKUs in case quantities. Track cost, store, receipt date and expiration.

2 · Bin + shelf storage

Dedicated shelf/bin location per SKU, with food separated from apparel and seasonal goods.

3 · Subscription assembly

Predefine $99, $179 and $299 carton recipes to reduce pick/pack time.

4 · Reorder rules

Set minimum stock, target weeks of supply and replenishment triggers before a SKU runs dry.
