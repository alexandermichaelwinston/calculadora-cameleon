A guide built around Houston

La Creme is an independent local discovery guide owned and operated by Michael Alexander. Our home is lacreme.app, and our focus is Houston: restaurants, tacos, date ideas, nightlife, art, music, markets and neighborhood favorites.

The guide brings editorial starting points, a map and community recommendations together so you can decide where to explore next. Search and directions links let you continue with an outside map service.

What “La Creme Heat” means

Our Heat and FIRE labels are La Creme’s own editorial selections. They are separate from a venue’s outside ratings, official awards or community chile scores. Where outside place data is available, it can add information to the guide; it does not make the outside provider an endorser of La Creme.

Some photographs are illustrative and show a mood or type of experience. They may not show the particular venue named on a card. Always confirm details with the business before visiting.

Local knowledge belongs here

Know a taco truck, music venue or date spot worth sharing? Community pins let visitors contribute a public place, category, chile rating and note. Contributions help broaden the guide and can be corrected or removed when needed.

Keep recommendations useful and respectful. Share public business information and leave personal or private details out of your note.

How the guide may be supported

We may introduce clearly identified affiliate links to help support the guide. An active affiliate link can earn a commission under the relevant program’s terms. Ordinary links to businesses do not mean those businesses have approved an affiliate application or endorsed La Creme.

As of October 12, 2026, the main lacreme.app guide has no active affiliate tracking integration. We will identify active affiliate links when introduced. Our Privacy Policy explains the related tracking and visitor choices.
