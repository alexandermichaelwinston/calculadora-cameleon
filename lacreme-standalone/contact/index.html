What can we help with?

Place or listing corrections
Include the place name, its La Creme page or section, and the detail that needs updating. A link to the business’s current information is helpful.
Privacy and personal information
Tell us whether you are requesting access, correction, deletion or help with another privacy concern. Include enough information to locate the relevant submission. See our Privacy Policy.
Community content
Report an inaccurate or inappropriate pin, or ask about a contribution you submitted. Include the place name and a short description.
Business and affiliate inquiries
Share your business name, website and proposal. An inquiry or a listing does not establish an affiliate relationship or endorsement.

Before you send

Community pins are public. Use email for privacy concerns and keep personal contact details out of public notes. Do not email passwords, payment-card details or identity documents.

For a purchase, reservation, delivery or refund made through an outside link, contact the merchant or booking provider directly. La Creme’s email is for questions about the guide.
