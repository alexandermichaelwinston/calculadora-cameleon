Yield Depot compares government money-market funds, Treasury-heavy cash vehicles, high-yield savings and ultra-short bond choices. The monthly preview explains what changed and where after-tax yield is most competitive. Money-market funds are investment funds, not FDIC-insured bank deposits.

DIVIDEND HUNTER™

High yield without blindly chasing yield

Rank a dividend-paying universe using trailing yield, consecutive dividend-growth years, payout ratio and a safety rating derived from payout ratio plus leverage.

Premium output: ticker, company, yield, growth streak, payout ratio, safety rating.

EARLYBIRD™ 100

Growth + value + momentum

Rank using revenue growth, EPS acceleration, forward-P/E discount, momentum and quality, normalized to 0–100 before weighting.

Premium output: ticker, company, EB Score, revenue growth, EPS acceleration, forward P/E.

FIXED SCHEDULE

Publish weekly, not on demand

The spec requires one fixed formula applied equally to the universe and publication on a regular schedule. No personalized “should I buy X?” output.
