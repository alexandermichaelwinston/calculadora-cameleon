# Learn & Earn Affiliate OS — Digital Asset Portfolio

## Core rule
Every asset must **solve a real problem first**, then capture permission, then show a small number of relevant partner options. Affiliate payout never determines recommendation order.

## Funnel architecture
1. **Free utility / lead magnet** — calculator, checklist, report, app, comparison tool, template, map or estimator.
2. **Permission-based capture** — email + explicit consent + interest tag.
3. **Immediate value** — deliver the promised tool/checklist before monetization pressure.
4. **Congruent partner ribbon** — 3–7 options tied directly to the user's current job-to-be-done.
5. **Tracking** — asset, placement, partner, campaign, click, lead, qualified lead, sale/revenue.
6. **Follow-up** — educational email sequence plus relevant tools/offers.
7. **Testing** — low-budget creative tests first; scale proven offers gradually.
8. **Owned offer graduation** — use winning affiliate demand to decide which digital product, API, marketplace, subscription or service to own.

## Tracking convention
Use consistent parameters on every approved commissionable link:

`utm_source=<asset>&utm_medium=affiliate-ribbon&utm_campaign=<intent>&utm_content=<partner>-<placement>`

Also store internal fields where possible:
- `asset`
- `placement`
- `partner`
- `intent`
- `creative_id`
- `lead_id` or anonymous session id
- `click_ts`
- `conversion_value`

## Network activation order
Start with broad networks so one approval unlocks many brands, then add direct programs where conversion economics justify the admin work.

1. ClickBank — digital products/offers.
2. impact.com — broad consumer + business brands.
3. Awin — commerce, travel, retail, services.
4. CJ — large-brand publisher partnerships.
5. PartnerStack — B2B SaaS and recurring software partnerships.
6. Direct partner programs — only after the asset has enough intent/traffic to justify direct relationship management.

## Offer scorecard
Score each possible partner 0–5 on:
- audience congruence
- trust / brand fit
- payout economics
- conversion friction
- recurring revenue potential
- refund / complaint risk
- compliance risk
- mobile experience
- API/deep-link capability
- reporting quality

Do not promote an offer just because commission is high. The best long-term offer is the one that improves user outcomes and retains trust.

## Creative testing rule
For each high-intent asset, create multiple visual hooks and headlines but send them to the same controlled funnel. Test small, measure conversion to lead and revenue, then scale gradually. Never jump spend aggressively after one good day.

## Owned-audience rule
Each useful tool should have an optional but prominent lead magnet tied to the tool's intent. The email list is a portfolio-level asset and should survive any single ad platform, social platform, search ranking or partner change.

## Disclosure standard
Use plain-language, conspicuous disclosure near commissionable recommendations, for example:

> We may earn a commission if you choose a partner through these links. It does not change what you pay, and compensation does not determine ranking.

Do not rely on vague labels such as only “affiliate link.”

## 30-day rollout
### Week 1
- standardize lead-capture forms
- enable Netlify Forms where appropriate
- add thumbnail ribbons
- define analytics naming
- apply to the five core networks

### Week 2
- activate 3–5 relevant partner programs per top asset
- replace application links/placeholders with approved tracked links
- publish the first lead magnets
- add disclosure text beside each live commissionable placement

### Week 3
- build 5–10 creative variants per top asset
- launch small paid/organic tests
- measure lead cost, click-through rate, conversion rate and earnings per visitor

### Week 4
- remove low-trust / low-conversion offers
- promote winners
- add email follow-up sequences
- identify which affiliate category should become an owned product, marketplace, API or enterprise service

## Graduation path
Affiliate revenue is market research. Once an asset proves a repeatable high-value customer need, move from:

**affiliate recommendation → lead-gen marketplace → owned digital offer → subscription/API → enterprise partnership**

That is how the portfolio compounds instead of staying a collection of outbound links.
