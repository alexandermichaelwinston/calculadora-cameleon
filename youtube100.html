For operators, not spectators

Two lenses. One North American opportunity.

🦎 El Camaleón | Americas

Exports are accelerating; the risks remain granular. Mexican manufactured exports, USMCA review uncertainty and the peso's response to U.S. data give cross-border entrepreneurs three reasons to model individual products rather than assume one national outcome.

  • Prioritize electronics, component sourcing, specialized logistics and supplier-development opportunities—but distinguish foreign content from Mexican value added.
  • Check actual USMCA qualification and tariff schedules before promising duty-free delivery.
  • Use a landed-cost model with FX, transport, utilities, customs brokers and quality control.

🤠 The Tall Texan | Texas

Make AI and industrial growth pay for themselves. Operator advantage comes from disciplined capital, documented construction workflows, bilingual crews and local supplier execution—not trend chasing.

  • For Alex Pros, standardize bilingual scopes and demonstrate built work with real jobsite photo proof.
  • For Real Deals, compare cash-yield durability against leveraged rental returns.
  • For AI and industrial service projects, measure task success, financing exposure, safety and labor retention.
