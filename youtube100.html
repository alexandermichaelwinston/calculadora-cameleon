Opportunity lenses

Two editions. One North American market.

🦎 El Camaleón | Americas

North American industrial supply chains are changing, but the tariff math comes first. New Mexico electrical-equipment investment, existing auto-parts capacity and shifting U.S. trade threats create a strong supplier-intelligence beat.

  • Track WEG and other announced plants separately from funded purchase orders and vendor registrations.
  • For every quote record product-specific HS code, current tariffs, USMCA origin proof and USD/MXN sensitivity.
  • Build a bilingual list of industrial suppliers with capacity, certifications, freight lanes and buyer contacts.

🤠 The Tall Texan | Texas

Win with better execution before relying on market recovery. Builders and investors can find opportunities in weak housing markets—but capital discipline, reliable crews, durable construction and supplier readiness remain decisive.

  • Screen rentals and flips for builder concessions, realistic insurance/tax bills, carrying costs and lease-up risk.
  • Require photographs of flashing/drainage and written field-office handoffs on renovations.
  • Test AI quote-to-invoice automation with human approvals; qualify for industrial work only after bids are real.
