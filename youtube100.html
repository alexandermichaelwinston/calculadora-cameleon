01AI
Oct 21, 2026 · Publisher episode

The Missing Infrastructure for AI Agents: Documents

a16z Show · Adit Abraham

Main idea. AI agents can reason well but still miss critical data in scanned documents, messy PDFs, tables and spreadsheets.

Why it matters. A missed line in a contract, insurance file or construction estimate can turn an impressive demo into a costly production error.

Monday move → Benchmark document extraction on 25 real bid sheets, contracts and invoices. Record omitted fields and require review before approvals or payments.

For operators, not spectators

Two lenses. One North American opportunity.

🦎 El Camaleón | Americas

Adapt across the border—with the numbers. The Mexican peso's movement, the industrial-park rollout and the USMCA policy debate all point to opportunity, but not a blanket permission slip to offshore manufacturing.

  • Compare landed cost with tariff classification, USMCA origin eligibility and USD/MXN scenarios.
  • Screen industrial zones for available utilities, qualified labor, logistics and verifiable incentives.
  • Offer cross-border supplier sourcing, bilingual B2B lead-generation and compliance-aware operating tools.

🤠 The Tall Texan | Texas

Win the second-order work. Industrial developments create potential contracts for Texas fabricators, framers, electricians, HVAC contractors, logistics providers and commercial landlords, but announcements are not guaranteed orders.

  • Prepare vendor onboarding, insurance, bonding and trade-specific capability statements for major projects.
  • Use documented change orders, photo QA and project dashboards to protect contractor gross margin.
  • Stress-test local rental cash flow instead of assuming Texas population growth resolves financing risk.
