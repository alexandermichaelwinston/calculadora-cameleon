Editorial lenses

🦎 El Camaleón Lens

This week rewards operators who can adapt across technology, capital and borders without confusing speed with recklessness.

  • AI infrastructure demand is real, but capital intensity means valuation discipline matters more, not less.
  • Mexico remains strategically important to North American manufacturing; rules of origin, documentation and contingency planning decide whether the economics survive.
  • Audience and relationship capital can reduce dependence on paid acquisition and outside gatekeepers.

🤠 Tall Texan Lens

Texas sits where AI power demand, construction, local capital, housing growth and Mexico-linked trade intersect.

  • Waco BTR is a reminder to underwrite Texas growth at the project level, not from statewide headlines.
  • Building-science details protect contractor margin by reducing callbacks and hidden moisture risk.
  • Local capital structures may become more important for community redevelopment and small-business growth where conventional financing leaves gaps.
