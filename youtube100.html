4 business ideas you could build with ChatGPT

My First Million

Main idea. The hosts explore focused AI apps for travel rewards, taxes and other specific problems.

02Real estate
Nov 25, 2026 · Publisher podcast / transcript

Why I’m Moving (More) Money Into Passive Real Estate in 2027

BiggerPockets · Paul Shannon

Main idea. An experienced sponsor and passive investor examines syndication structures, distressed commercial property and sponsor selection; a rebound is his thesis, not a certainty.

Why it matters. Passive ownership reduces management work but adds illiquidity and sponsor risk.

05Mexico trade

EU propone nuevos aranceles a 14 socios comerciales por exceso de capacidad

El Economista

Main idea. A Spanish-language report covers proposed U.S. duties aimed at excess industrial capacity among trading partners.

Why it matters. New trade barriers could change relative sourcing economics; proposals are not effective tariff schedules and Mexico-specific exposure needs separate verification.

El Economista
