01AI
Nov 18, 2026 · Recorded contractor webinar

Lead to Profit: Use AI and JobTread to Turn Marketing Dollars Into Booked Jobs

JobTread · Scott Schaper (RSM Marketing)

Main idea. Missed calls, delayed follow-up, scattered CRM records and weak attribution waste contractor ad budgets. The webinar connects marketing, sales and job-cost data to booked work.

Why it matters. For Alex Pros and Rocket-Launcher.io, recovering existing inquiries can be more profitable than increasing ad spend.

Practical takeaway → Audit the last 50 inquiries: response time, estimate issued, booked job and gross profit. Pilot supervised missed-call follow-up and an estimate-to-job dashboard.

02Real estate
Nov 20, 2026 · Real-estate podcast / video

Home Prices Just Ended a 13-Year Streak | November Housing Update

BiggerPockets · Dave Meyer

Main idea. Dave Meyer argues that a slight national year-over-year price decline, slow transactions and higher borrowing costs are improving buyer leverage without necessarily producing a 2008-style forced-sale crash.

Why it matters. Texas markets differ sharply by ZIP code and builder competition; falling ask prices do not rescue bad holding costs.

Practical takeaway → For each Real Deals buy box, record closed-sale discounts, active inventory, concessions, insurance, taxes and 12-month carry. Run a second case with 5% lower resale value.

03Real estate
Nov 18, 2026 · Publisher transcript / podcast

(Please) Don’t Bet on a Refinance

BiggerPockets · Michael Zuber, Dave Meyer, Henry Washington

Main idea. The guests argue that a rental should work at today's financing terms instead of relying on later refinancing or appreciation; property type changes vacancy and maintenance assumptions.

Why it matters. Owner finance and conventional rentals alike can become cash traps when optimistic rates or generic expense estimates hide negative cash flow.

Practical takeaway → Approve offers on present-day debt service and reserves. Treat future refinancing as upside only; maintain different expense models for single-family, 2–4 unit and apartment assets.

The AI Bubble Already Popped

09Mexico trade

El Peso Se Depreció 2.26% y Sheinbaum Dialoga con Trump en APEC

Opportunity lenses

Two editions. One North American market.

🦎 El Camaleón | Americas

Trade wins come from landed-cost clarity, not country slogans. Peso volatility plus recent reports of softer Mexican manufacturing output create a supplier-by-supplier opportunity, not a blanket nearshoring boom.

  • Build bilingual qualified-supplier records: process, certifications, capacity, quote currency, lead time and freight lane.
  • Stress-test USD/MXN, origin compliance, duties and landed delivery price; tariff proposals are not enacted rates.
  • Editorial context: Mexico manufacturing report, November 20.

🤠 The Tall Texan | Texas

Cash flow before growth; conversion before more ad spend. Buyers may have leverage in some local markets, but asset-level carrying costs and contractor execution still determine whether that leverage pays.

  • For Real Deals, demand a workable current-rate scenario with reserve and price-cut stress tests.
  • For Alex Pros, connect lead-response speed to won jobs and gross profit—not vanity click counts.
  • For manufacturers, inspect factory automation ROI and procurement commitments before chasing announced plant spend.
