Opportunity lenses

Two editions. One North American market.

🦎 El Camaleón | Americas

Manufacturing, money and markets are connected. Mexico's trade outlook, peso movements and Banxico rate policy affect different supply chains differently. Avoid claiming every factory qualifies for tariff-free treatment.

  • Screen Texas–Mexico–Canada supplier corridors using actual product HS codes and documented USMCA origin.
  • Use dollar/peso scenarios and compare dollar debt with peso debt before quoting manufacturing costs.
  • Verify industrial park operations, customs costs, incentives and foreign-trade-zone eligibility rather than relying on headlines.

🤠 The Tall Texan | Texas

Protect profits before pursuing growth. Housing-policy changes, capable contractors and industrial traceability create practical opportunities, but each requires evidence and execution.

  • Build documented before/after video marketing and eliminate critical single-person operating dependencies.
  • Underwrite Texas ADUs and rental projects using current local rules, insurance, property taxes and financing.
  • Identify suppliers to grid, housing and industrial projects, but separate announced plans from available purchase orders.
