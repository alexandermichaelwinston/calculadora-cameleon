Storage model

Start with a compact organized warehouse or flex-space setup rather than scattered home inventory.

  • Food-safe shelving and labeled bins
  • SKU/bin location map
  • FIFO rotation for dated food
  • Separate apparel/seasonal storage
  • Standard carton sizes for subscriptions
  • Receiving table + packing stations

Inventory rules

The objective is availability without tying up excessive cash.

  • Stock proven subscription staples deeper
  • Use reorder points by weeks of supply
  • Track expiration and sell-through
  • Bundle slower SKUs instead of discounting individually
  • Pre-build subscription recipes before pack day
  • Reserve capacity for $500+ corporate orders
