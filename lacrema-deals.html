La Crema affiliate playbook

  1. Build “Best bottle under $20/$30/$50” and “date-night pairing” pages around high-intent searches.
  2. Show a clear “affiliate link” disclosure beside every monetized outbound link and keep editorial rankings independent of commission.
  3. Use one canonical partner-link endpoint so affiliate IDs can be swapped without editing every article.
  4. Track clicks by partner, category and article with UTM parameters and a first-party analytics event.
  5. Combine restaurant discovery with wine pairing content: restaurant → cuisine → recommended bottle → nearby retailer / online merchant.
  6. Create member-savings guides for Costco, Sam’s Club and Total Wine rewards programs, then convert the best-performing guides into direct sponsorship pitches.
