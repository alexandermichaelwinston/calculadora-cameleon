La Crema affiliate playbook

  1. Publish high-intent Texas lifestyle and food guides that can naturally feature merch, wine and membership savings.
  2. Show a clear affiliate/sponsored disclosure beside monetized outbound links and keep La Crema editorial rankings independent of commission.
  3. Use one canonical partner-link endpoint so approved affiliate IDs can be swapped without editing every article.
  4. Track partner clicks, article source, restaurant source and conversion-oriented events with UTM parameters and first-party analytics.
  5. Connect restaurant discovery to commerce: restaurant → cuisine → pairing → nearby retailer / online merchant.
  6. Turn the highest-traffic savings guides into direct sponsorship pitches for Total Wine, wineries, grocers and local event partners.
  7. Build sponsor packages around measurable deliverables: featured placement, Houston map pin, pairing article, newsletter slot, event promotion and post-campaign click report.
